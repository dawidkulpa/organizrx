# Welcome to OrganizrX

OrganizrX is the modern, high-performance successor to the original Organizr. Rebuilt from the ground up using TypeScript and Bun, it serves as your ultimate media server dashboard, bringing all your services together into a single, cohesive interface.

Whether you are managing a complex home lab or just want a cleaner way to access your media services, OrganizrX provides the tools to organize, secure, and streamline your digital life.

## Key Features

- **Unified Dashboard:** Access all your favorite services through customizable tabs.
- **Modern Tech Stack:** Built with Hono, React, and Bun for lightning-fast performance and stability.
- **Advanced Authentication:** Secure your dashboard with local accounts, Plex OAuth, LDAP, or OIDC.
- **Plugin System:** Extend functionality with built-in and community-developed plugins for media management, downloaders, and more.
- **Multi-Database Support:** Choose between SQLite, MySQL, or PostgreSQL to suit your infrastructure.
- **Easy Migration:** Seamlessly transition from Organizr v2 while preserving your essential data.
- **Responsive Design:** A beautiful UI built with Tailwind CSS v4 that looks great on desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.
