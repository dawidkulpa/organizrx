# Bookmarks

Bookmarks in OrganizrX provide a convenient way to store and organize links to external sites and services that you don't need as full sidebar tabs. They are perfect for grouping related links into custom categories.

## Bookmark Categories

Like tabs, bookmarks can be grouped into categories for better organization.

1. Navigate to **Settings > Bookmarks > Categories**.
2. **Create Category:** Click the button to add a new category.
3. **Name:** The label that will appear in the bookmark group.
4. **Icon:** Assign a unique icon to the category for visual clarity.
5. **Color:** (Optional) Choose a color for the category title or background.

Categories allow you to quickly find related links without cluttering your sidebar with many individual tabs.

## Creating and Organizing Bookmarks

To manage your bookmarks, navigate to **Settings > Bookmarks > Tabs**.

1. **Add Bookmark:** Click the button to add a new link.
2. **Name:** The display name for the bookmark.
3. **URL:** The link to the external service or site.
4. **Category:** Choose the category where this bookmark belongs.
5. **Icon:** Select an icon or provide a custom URL for the bookmark.

## Group Permissions

You can control which user groups can see specific bookmark categories and individual links.

- **Category Permissions:** Hide entire groups of bookmarks from specific users (e.g., restricted links for regular users).
- **Tab Permissions:** (Coming soon) Finer-grained control over individual bookmarks.

This ensures that only authorized users can access the links you've stored in your OrganizrX dashboard.

## Ordering and Icons

OrganizrX makes it easy to customize your bookmarks:

- **Drag and Drop:** Reorder bookmarks and categories within the settings page to change their display order.
- **Icon Library:** Choose from a wide range of built-in icons to represent your bookmarks.
- **Custom CSS:** (Optional) Use CSS overrides to further personalize the look of your bookmark groups.

Bookmarks are a powerful tool for extending your OrganizrX dashboard with all the links you need for your daily media management.
